Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026. Hushclaw is made by Vantward Games.

Playing solo

Solo play needs no account. Your settings, best score, rank and this computer's leaderboard are saved only on your computer. Nothing about solo play is sent to us.

Playing online

Online play needs a free Hushclaw account. To run co-op, teams and the online leaderboards we store:

  • your username and an account ID;
  • the country you choose (you can change it at any time);
  • your team, if you create or join one;
  • your online match results: score, time played, kills and the island seed.

Your password is held by Unity's sign-in service. We never see it.

Your username, team and scores appear on the public leaderboards.

Voice chat

Voice chat goes live between the players in your match. It is not recorded or stored. The game measures how loud you are so the dinosaurs in your match can react; only that loudness level is shared with the players in your match.

Who processes it

Accounts, co-op connections, voice chat and leaderboards run on Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies, which processes this data for us. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Email support@hushclaw.games from any address, with your username, and we will delete your account, team membership and scores. You can also ask us what we hold about you.

Children

Hushclaw is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
