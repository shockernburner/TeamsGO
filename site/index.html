Co-op dinosaur survival where the dinosaurs can hear you.
Drop in, salvage, survive, get out together.

Play the free alpha on itch.io Steam page coming soon

Windows and macOS. 1 to 4 players.

In some parallel universe, the dinosaurs never died out. Humanity pushed them back to a ring of islands beyond the Cordon and called it peace. On the islands, they changed. They learned our engines. Our lights. Our voices.

You are a TETHER team: one to four survivors dropped onto a lost island to salvage what the dead outposts left behind, then fire a flare and climb the helicopter ladder before the island takes you.

They can hear you

Your real voice carries in the world. Whisper and a raptor walks past your hiding spot. Talk too loud and something turns its head. Shout to pull a hunter off a teammate.

  • A new island every match: jungle, plains, swamp and volcanic ground, rivers and lakes.
  • An apex hunter that tracks your scent, and an island that fights back with stampedes, packs and storms.
  • Co-op for 1 to 4 with a join code, proximity voice chat, and revives for downed teammates.
  • Form or join a team and climb the Survivors and Teams leaderboards, worldwide or by country.

Stay quiet. Stay close. Leave no one.
