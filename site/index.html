Co-op dinosaur survival where the dinosaurs can hear you. Drop in, salvage, survive, get out together.

The story

In some parallel universe, the dinosaurs never died out. Humanity pushed them back to a ring of islands beyond the Cordon and called it peace. On the islands, they changed. They learned our engines. Our lights. Our voices.

You are a TETHER salvage team: one to four survivors dropped onto a lost island to recover what the dead outposts left behind, then fire a flare and climb the helicopter ladder before the island takes you.

They can hear you

Your real voice carries in the world. Whisper and a raptor walks past your hiding spot. Talk too loud and something turns its head. Shout to pull a hunter off a teammate.

  • A new island every match, generated from a seed: jungle, plains, swamp and volcanic ground, rivers and lakes.
  • Ironjaw, an apex hunter that tracks your scent.
  • An island that fights back: stampedes, packs, storms, and the hunter sent at you as the clock runs down.
  • Co-op for 1 to 4 with a join code, proximity voice, and reviving downed teammates.
  • Form or join a team, pick its emblem, and climb the Survivors and Teams leaderboards, worldwide or by country.

Early alpha

Hushclaw is in early development. Windows and macOS. Expect rough edges, and tell us what breaks on the itch.io page. On macOS the app isn't notarized yet: if it says it can't be opened, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and press Open Anyway. On Windows, SmartScreen may ask you to press More info > Run anyway.
