# Recording the intro narration

Ten short lines, about ten minutes of work. The game plays them automatically once they are in place.

## The lines (read exactly as written)

| File | Line |
|---|---|
| Line01 | In some parallel universe, the dinosaurs never died out. |
| Line02 | They ruled the Earth for sixty-six million more years. |
| Line03 | Then we came. Small, clever, and afraid of the dark. |
| Line04 | We built fire. Then walls. Then cities. And we pushed them back to the sea. |
| Line05 | The last of them were sealed on islands beyond the Cordon. We called it peace. |
| Line06 | But on the islands, they changed. They learned our engines. Our lights. Our voices. |
| Line07 | One by one, our outposts went silent. |
| Line08 | Now we send crews of four to bring back what was left. And get out before dark. |
| Line09 | Stay quiet. Stay close. Leave no one. |
| Line10 | They can hear you. |

## How to read them

- Low, calm, unhurried, like telling a story at a campfire. Not a movie-trailer shout.
- About two words a second. Take a real pause at every full stop.
- Lines 9 and 10 get quieter. Line 10 is almost a whisper.
- Leave half a second of silence before and after each line.

## Setup

- A quiet room with soft things around (a closet of clothes or a bed with a duvet works well). Turn off fans and the AC.
- Phone voice memo app or a headset mic, about a hand's width (15 cm) from your mouth, slightly to the side so breaths don't pop.
- Do three takes of each line and keep the best one.

## Files

- One file per line, named `Line01` to `Line10`.
- WAV, M4A, MP3 or OGG are all fine; Unity imports any of them.
- Lines can be longer than the on-screen time: each card waits for its voice to finish, and the music drops under the voice.

## Where they go

`Assets/_Project/Resources/Story/Voice/Line01.wav` … `Line10.wav`.
Upload them to the project or drop them in a folder on the Mac, and they get placed there. They are your own recordings, so they are committed like any other project file.

To hear the result in the game: Settings → Play intro.
