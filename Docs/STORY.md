# TETHER: Primal — story and intro

## The world in one paragraph

Sixty-six million years ago the asteroid missed. The dinosaurs never died out, and humans arrived late: small,
clever and afraid of the dark. Over thousands of years people pushed the great animals back with fire, walls and
finally cities, until the last of them were sealed onto a ring of islands beyond **the Cordon**, a line of sea
forts and treaties everyone called peace. On the islands the animals changed. Crowded and hunted, they grew
smarter. They learned the sound of engines, the meaning of lights, and the human voice. Research outposts went
quiet one by one, and coastal towns started hearing roars at night. The Cordon is failing.

## Who the players are

**TETHER crews.** Four-person salvage teams (one to four players) flown in by helicopter to islands the Cordon has
lost. Their job: recover what the dead outposts left behind (supply caches, ruins, data), survive the island, and
fire a rescue flare so the helicopter can lift them out on its ladder. The name comes from the drop line every crew
clips onto on the way in: you go down together, you come up together.

The three crew rules, painted inside every helicopter door:

1. Stay quiet.
2. Stay close.
3. Leave no one.

## How it fits the game

- **Islands are procedural** because the Cordon keeps losing new ones; every match is a different island.
- **Dinosaurs hear your voice** because they learned to: whisper and they pass by, shout and they come.
- **Ironjaw** is the apex hunter the crews name after its bite; it follows scent.
- **The Threat Director** is the island itself turning on you. In team-vs-team (later), rival salvage outfits
  compete for the same island and fire **lure beacons** that draw threats onto each other's crews.
- **Score and rank** are your crew's salvage record; leaderboards rank the best crews and survivors.
- **Future TETHER games** use the same idea (a crew of four, tied together, getting out of a disaster): underwater
  (Deep), wildfire (Blaze), crash site (Wreck), team racing (Rally).
